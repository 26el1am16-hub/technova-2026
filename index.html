FAQ

Who can participate?

Any student with a valid college email ID. Students from all departments and colleges are welcome.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Registration for all events is free (dummy information for this demo).

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes, as long as the event timings do not clash. You need a separate registration for each event.

How big can a team be?

It depends on the event. The maximum team size is listed on every event card.

I lost my registration ID. What now?

Use the My Registrations section and search with your college email.

Can I change my details after registering?

Contact the organisers using the details below and quote your registration ID.
